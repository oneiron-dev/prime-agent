- A session whose worker process dies while starting (its socket cannot be bound, it crashes at boot, it dies mid-handshake) no longer leaves the interactive client waiting: the daemon answers the open as soon as the worker exits, and `prime-agent` restores the terminal, prints the worker's exit status, its error line and its log path, and exits with status 1. The daemon reports it as the typed `worker_startup_failed` refusal (daemon schema 31, `worker_startup_failure` capability) and leaves nothing of the failed launch behind.
